Sweet Spot - Psychology, Retention & Monetization Strategy
 1. The Core Engine: Nir Eyal's "Hook Model"
The entire app experience is designed around Nir Eyal's Hook Model to transform poker  study from a chore into an addictive daily habit:
Trigger: An external cue, such as a scheduled push notification ("Your Daily  •  Challenge is waiting").
Action: A frictionless behavior—opening the app and spending 3 minutes solving a  •  fast-paced poker spot.
Variable Reward: The unpredictability of the challenge. The user doesn't know  • what the spot will be, how hard it is, or exactly how many Gold Coins they 
 will win. (Daily Challenge awards Gold Coins only — never Elo or XP; see mvp.md.)
Investment: The user invests time in building their hidden Elo profile, progressing  •  on the map, and accumulating a daily "Streak" that they will want to protect.
2.	Engagement Targets
 Total Daily Target: 10-15 minutes maximum. •
 Session Length: 3-5 minutes per session. •
Psychological Goal: Create a lightweight daily habit. The app must feel like a fun  •  break rather than a heavy educational platform that causes burnout.
3.	Retention & User Psychology
Dynamic Visual Environments (Gamified Learning): To prevent visual fatigue and  • keep the gameplay exciting, progression uses the four canonical Worlds from mvp.md (Benny's Garden, Local Casino, VIP Room, Final Table — World 4 deferred). Light/Dark is a day/night treatment of the same location, not separate fantasy skins (no wild west / cyberpunk variants).
Illusion of Control: Within MVP, progression is sequential stage unlock (see mvp.md)  • rather than a fully open branching map. Later UX may add limited choice between topics; do not build an open multi-branch map in early sprints.
Loss Aversion (The Streak Effect): Tracking consecutive daily logins (e.g., a "14-Day  •
Fire" badge). The human brain hates losing what it already has. Users will log in  just to avoid resetting their hard-earned streak.
The Zeigarnik Effect (Unfinished Business): Using visual progress bars on the map  •
(e.g., "Casino Heist World: 82% Complete"). The brain dislikes open loops, creating  a strong subconscious urge to return to the app and hit 100%.
 4. Sensory Psychology (Audio-Visual Feedback)
The app utilizes "Casino Psychology" to trigger dopamine release and keep the user  hooked through sensory cues:
Visual Dopamine (Colors): Utilizing a Dark Mode base to make interactive  • elements pop. Saturated colors are used functionally: Neon Green/Gold for correct answers and "Sweet Spots" (signaling wealth), and bright Red for mistakes  (creating an immediate aversion to failure).
 Auditory Cues (Sound Design): •
Positive Reinforcement: High-pitched, ascending chimes for correct  o  answers to instantly trigger a dopamine hit.
The Jackpot Effect: Completing a stage triggers a prolonged cascade of  o casino chips clinking, tricking the brain into feeling a massive sense of 
 reward.
Adrenaline/Focus: Using heavy, suspenseful sounds of an "All-In" moment  o
(like a heartbeat or intense chip shuffling) during time-pressure spots to  physically raise the user's heart rate and force hyper-focus.
 5. Monetization Strategy (Freemium Hybrid)
A.	Microtransactions (Friction Reduction & Status)
The Chip Stack (Lives System): Instead of generic "hearts" or "lives", the user starts  • with a stack of 3 Poker Chips. If they fail too many spots, they lose their chips. They can wait 12 hours for a free refill, or pay a microtransaction for an instant  "Rebuy" to keep playing.
Cosmetics & Status: Users can purchase premium skins for their "Chip Stack" (e.g.,  • diamond chips, flaming chips), premium card backs, and unique avatars to show 
 off their status on the leaderboards.
B.	Premium Subscription (Sweet Spot Pro) Designed for users who want to treat the app  like a personal coach.
Hidden Data Unlocked: Free users only see their map progression. Premium users  •  unlock a personal statistical dashboard.
Weakness Analysis: The app reveals exactly where the user is losing Elo (e.g.,  •  "Blind Defense: 32% Success").
Targeted AI Training: Premium users can ask the AI engine to generate custom  •  practice sessions focusing only on their specific leaks.
 6. Future Features (Parking Lot)
The GTO Lab: A premium tool where users can input real hands they played with  • friends in the real world and receive an instant algorithmic analysis (GTO) of their 
 exact mathematical mistake.
